# How to update your website

Everything on your site can be changed from your iPad. There's no code, nothing to install, and you can't break it. 💫

---

## Getting in

1. Open **Safari** and go to **becca-worthington.sanity.studio**
2. Sign in (Google or email, whichever you set up)
3. **Tip:** tap the Share button → **Add to Home Screen**. You'll get an app icon for your site editor.

## The menu

On the left (or behind the ☰ button) you'll see:

| Menu item | What's in it |
|---|---|
| **Home page** | Your headshot slideshow, bio, and the sections below it |
| **Resume & headshots** | Resume PDF, downloadable headshots, your credits |
| **Reels & videos** | All your videos |
| **Site settings** | Name, tagline, **color theme**, email, agents, social links |
| **Projects** | One page per project (Channeling Our Ancestors, etc.) |

## Live preview: see your real website while you edit

The editor opens on **Live preview**: your actual website, next to the editing panel.

- **Tap any text or photo** on the website to edit it. The matching box opens on the side.
- **Changes show up in the preview within a second or two** as you type. That's a preview just for you; visitors don't see anything until you Publish.
- Use the **address bar at the top of the preview** (or tap the menu on the site) to go to another page, like Resume or Reels.
- On the Home page you can **drag sections** up and down right in the preview. On the Reels page you can drag videos.
- On an iPad held upright the screen is narrow, so tap the **phone / tablet / desktop** buttons above the preview to switch how it's shown, or turn the iPad sideways.

Prefer plain forms? Tap **Edit content** in the top bar.

## The golden rule: Publish

Changes are saved as a **draft** automatically while you type. Nothing goes live until you tap **Publish** (bottom right).
After publishing, the live site updates in **about a minute**.

Made a mistake? Open the **⋯ menu** next to Publish to discard changes or go back to an earlier version.

## Common things

### Add a new credit
**Resume & headshots** → **Credits** → open a section (e.g. *Theatre*) → **+ Add item** → fill in show, role, theatre → **Publish**.
To reorder credits, drag them by the ⋮⋮ handle on the left.

### Update your resume PDF
**Resume & headshots** → **Resume PDF** → tap it → **Replace** / **Upload** → pick the file from the Files app → **Publish**.

### Add or swap headshots
**Home page** → **Headshots at the top** (for the slideshow), or **Resume & headshots** → **Headshots** (for downloads).
Tap **+ Add item** → **Upload** → choose from Photos. Fill in "Describe the photo" (helps people using screen readers and helps Google).
To choose what part of the photo stays visible when it's cropped, tap the photo → the crop icon → drag the circle onto your face.

### Add a video
In the **YouTube app**: open the video → **Share** → **Copy link**.
Then **Reels & videos** → **Videos** → **+ Add item** → paste the link, add a title → **Publish**.
Turn on **"Show on the Home page"** to feature it on the front page. The **first** video in the list is shown biggest.

### Announce a show
**Home page** → **Page sections** → open **Now Playing** (or tap **+ Add item → Upcoming / now playing**) → add the show, dates, and a ticket link → **Publish**.
When it closes, delete the show, or turn on **"Hide this section for now"**.

### Add a press quote
**Home page** → **Page sections** → **Press** → **Quotes** → **+ Add item**.

### Add a project page
**Projects** → **+** (top) → fill in the title → tap **Generate** next to "Web address" → add a summary, cover image, links → **Publish**.
Turn on **"Feature on the Home page"** to show it on the front page.

### Change the colors
**Site settings** → **Look & feel** tab → choose a theme → **Publish**.

### Rearrange the Home page
**Home page** → **Page sections** → drag sections up and down by the ⋮⋮ handle. Use **+ Add item** to add a new one (gallery, text block, press, etc.).
Each section has **"Hide this section for now"** so you can switch it off without deleting it.

### Update your agents or socials
**Site settings** → **Contact & agents** or **Social links** tab.

## Good to know

- **Photos:** upload straight from your phone or camera roll. The site resizes them automatically, so there's no need to shrink them first.
- **Anything in [square brackets]** is a placeholder waiting for your real info.
- **Your domain** (becca-worthington.com) renews every year around **February 24**. Keep auto-renew on!
- **Need a bigger change?** Like a new layout, a new kind of section, or a new page? That's a developer job. The `README.md` file in the code explains everything to whoever helps you.
